Our Mission

Growing in Faith and Friendship

Guided by the patronage of Saint Carlo Acutis, the Catholic Youth Society reaches across every distance to form a joyful, nationwide family centered on Jesus Christ. Rooted in the Eucharist and united in faith, we work to heal community divides, encourage deep prayer, and build lasting friendships—empowering young people to live with hope, conviction, and holiness wherever they are.

Studies and Meetings

The Catholic Youth Society brings teens together nationwide through our vibrant virtual chapter—our central home for faith, prayer, and community. We gather weekly for scripture studies and live rosaries, host engaging monthly chapter meetings, and offer online events that help young people encounter Christ, deepen their faith, and build lasting friendships from anywhere in the country.
