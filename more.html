About CYS

Catholic Youth Society

Faith that connects.

The Catholic Youth Society is a national virtual Catholic youth group created to connect young people ages 11–18 through faith, friendship, prayer, service, and community.

CYS exists to help young Catholics encounter Christ, grow deeper in their faith, and build authentic friendships with other young people from across the country.

Leading With Faith

Guided by purpose
Guided By Purpose

Leading with faith.

At the Catholic Youth Society, our leaders are dedicated to building a welcoming, vibrant, nationwide family where every young person feels known, supported, and valued. We believe that true leadership starts with serving others and walking together on the path to holiness.

Inspired by the example of Saint Carlo Acutis, our team is passionate about mentoring youth, fostering deep friendships, and helping the next generation encounter the love of Christ in their daily lives.

“Let no one have contempt for your youth, but set an example for those who believe, in speech, conduct, love, faith, and purity.”
1 Timothy 4:12
