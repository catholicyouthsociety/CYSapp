Welcome to the CYS Community

This is a place to encourage one another, pray for one another, and help each other grow closer to Christ. Treat every person here with the dignity and charity they deserve.

🛡️
Community Safety

A community built with care.

CYS is designed around the safety, dignity, privacy, and well-being of young people. Community features should always be carefully moderated and never require members to share unnecessary personal information.

Respect Treat every member with Christian charity and dignity.
Privacy Don't share private information publicly or request it from others.
Moderation Community content should be reviewed and moderated appropriately.
